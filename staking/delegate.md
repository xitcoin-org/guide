# Delegate XTC

Delegation assigns stake to a validator while the delegator retains ownership of the delegation.

## Before delegating

* verify the Cosmos chain ID;
* confirm the validator operator address from live chain data;
* review commission and signing performance;
* keep enough XTC available for transaction fees;
* understand the 21-day unbonding period.

Delegation, redelegation and undelegation are on-chain transactions. Confirm every field in the wallet before signing.
