# Risks and slashing

Validators must sign blocks reliably and must never sign conflicting blocks.

The staking configuration includes penalties for downtime and double-signing. Delegators share validator performance risk.

## Reduce risk

* diversify operational dependencies;
* maintain secure backups and signing-key procedures;
* monitor missed blocks and peer connectivity;
* never restore a consensus key with an incorrect signing state;
* review validator history before delegating.

Exact live parameters should be queried from the active network.
