Do not recolor the supplied artwork to force a match.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep the complete square canvas and leave visible space between the mark
and adjacent text, controls or other logos. Nothing should overlap the symbol.

No numeric clear-space ratio or minimum display size is established by the
approved source package. The 32-pixel export is an available asset, not a
minimum-size standard. Choose enough pixels for the intended display density
and preview the mark at its final size. Request approved source artwork if
it is not legible.

## Background and contrast

Use a quiet background that separates the symbol from surrounding content.
Preview light and dark themes and avoid busy photography or patterns behind
the mark. Preserve transparency in the standard PNG files.

The legacy `white` files include a white background; they are not dark-theme
reverse marks. Do not remove their background or invent a monochrome variant.
Logo colors do not establish accessible text or control colors: assess those
interface elements separately.
