# Testnet and risk notice

The Xitcoin testnet is experimental infrastructure.

* Testnet XTC has no monetary value.
* Testnet state may be reset.
* Endpoints may be unavailable or rate-limited.
* Parameters can change between releases.
* Mainnet has not launched.
* A successful testnet transaction does not guarantee mainnet behavior.

Nothing in this guide is financial, legal, tax or investment advice. Users and operators are responsible for securing keys, verifying network identifiers and assessing software risk.
