# Legacy resources

Earlier Xitcoin material focused on the Cronos token, liquidity, staking farms and planned network concepts. Those pages are retained in Git history for traceability but are not used as current blockchain documentation.

Legacy references may be restored only after their contracts, links, availability and risk disclosures have been revalidated.

The canonical current architecture is the Cosmos SDK, CometBFT and Cosmos EVM proof-of-stake chain documented in this guide.
